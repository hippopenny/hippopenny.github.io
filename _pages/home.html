    What changes when agents act

    A wrong answer is annoying. A wrong action is a real problem.

    An agent that pays the wrong invoice, books the wrong time, or opens the wrong record does real damage. Once AI can take actions, the hard questions change.

    Whose permission is it acting on?

    An agent should only be able to do what the person it works for could do.

    How much can it spend?

    Every agent needs a budget it can't go over.

    What did it actually do?

    Every action should leave a record you can check, and a way to stop or undo it.

    These aren't extra features. They're what decides whether agents get trusted with real work at all.

    01 · Money and data

    Agents that touch money and data

    Businesses want agents working inside their systems, but can't hand over the keys without limits. We're working on how agents get the right permissions, budgets, and audit trails, built into the systems, not just the prompt.

    In practiceAgentHippo gives each agent the permissions of the person it's acting for, checks a spend budget before every model call, and keeps a record of every action, enforced at the data layer. agenthippo.ai ↗
